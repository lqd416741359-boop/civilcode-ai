# Contracts / 合同编 — issue map

## Main issue clusters
- Contract formation: offer/acceptance and other formation mechanisms.
- Standard terms, notices and incorporation issues.
- Validity interface with General Provisions.
- Performance, simultaneous/anticipatory defenses where applicable, assignment/assumption, preservation of claims.
- Modification, rescission/termination and consequences.
- Breach: continued performance, cure, replacement/repair, damages, liquidated damages, deposits and other remedies where applicable.
- Named contracts such as sale, supply/utilities, gift, loan, guarantee, lease, financing lease, factoring, construction, carriage, technology, custody/warehousing, mandate, property services, partnership and others.
- Quasi-contract areas including management without mandate and unjust enrichment.

## Intake facts
Parties; contract/document/chat; subject matter; price/principal; payment/delivery/performance dates; conditions; standard terms; breach event; notices/demands; termination communications; losses; mitigation; guarantee/security; later acknowledgments/settlement.

## Private lending checklist
Do not infer a loan solely from a bank/WeChat/Alipay transfer. Check loan intent, principal, actual delivery, interest agreement, maturity, repayments, acknowledgments, relationship/background, guarantee/security and limitation.

## Amount analysis
Separate principal, contract price, restitution, interest, liquidated damages, compensatory damages and costs. The legally permissible rate or adjustment standard is a legal question and must be verified; the calculator only performs arithmetic.

## Cautions
- Distinguish contract formation, validity, effectiveness, performance and breach.
- A contract clause is not automatically enforceable merely because it was signed.
- Consumer, financial, housing, construction and platform transactions may trigger other statutes/regulations.
