# Personality Rights / 人格权编 — issue map

## Main issue clusters
- Life, body and health interests.
- Name and entity-name interests where applicable.
- Portrait rights.
- Reputation and honor.
- Privacy and private life/private information.
- Personal information protection under the Civil Code, together with applicable special legislation.

## Intake facts
What content/data/image/name was collected, used or disclosed; by whom; consent or other asserted basis; original context; audience/scope; platform/account; duration; screenshots/URLs/timestamps; requests to remove/correct; actual harm/loss; commercial use; minors or sensitive circumstances where relevant.

## Evidence
Preserve original URLs, account identifiers, timestamps, full-context screenshots/recordings, messages requesting removal/correction, platform notices, contracts/consent records, and evidence of dissemination/harm. Consider lawful notarized/electronic evidence preservation when proportionate and available.

## Cross-routing
- Personal information: check current Personal Information Protection Law and other applicable special rules.
- Online infringement/platform roles: check current Civil Code/tort rules and relevant judicial interpretations/special laws.

## Cautions
Do not treat criticism, false factual allegations, privacy disclosure, portrait use and personal-data processing as the same cause of action. Identify the protected interest and elements separately.
