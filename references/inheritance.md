# Inheritance / 继承编 — issue map

## Main issue clusters
- What constitutes the decedent's estate.
- Statutory succession and order/scope of heirs.
- Testamentary succession and legacies.
- Wills: form, capacity, authenticity, revocation/conflict and effectiveness.
- Legacy-support agreements where applicable.
- Renunciation, disqualification and special allocation considerations where applicable.
- Estate administration, debts/taxes/expenses, division and registration/transfer.

## Intake facts
Date/place of death; marital status; family tree; possible heirs; property/debts; ownership/title; spouse/co-owner interests; wills and versions; witnesses; video/audio/notarial materials; gifts/transfers before death; support arrangements; estate controller; funeral/estate expenses; disputes or prior proceedings.

## Mandatory sequence
1. Identify property actually owned by the decedent.
2. Separate spouse/co-owner property from the estate.
3. Identify debts/obligations and administration issues.
4. Check valid testamentary/legacy-support arrangements.
5. Only then analyze statutory succession for remaining estate.

## Cautions
Do not divide an entire jointly owned asset as if all of it were the estate. A document titled “will” is not automatically valid; verify statutory form and surrounding facts under current law and interpretations.
