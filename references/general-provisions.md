# General Provisions / 总则编 — issue map

Load this file for cross-cutting civil-law questions.

## Main issue clusters
- Civil subjects: natural persons, legal persons, unincorporated organizations.
- Capacity for civil rights and civil conduct; guardianship where relevant.
- Civil juristic acts: formation, expression of intent, validity, conditional/time-limited acts.
- Invalid / voidable acts and consequences: fraud, coercion, material mistake, exploitation/unconscionability and other statutory grounds require precise current-law verification.
- Agency: authority, unauthorized agency, apparent agency, ratification, termination.
- Civil rights and general civil liability principles.
- Limitation periods, suspension/interruption and other timing rules.

## Intake facts
Identify age/capacity where relevant; who acted; for whom; authority documents; what was said/signed; whether consent was genuine; dates of knowledge, performance and dispute; ratification/acceptance; and any later acknowledgment.

## Analysis cautions
- Do not label an act invalid merely because it is unfair or undesirable.
- Separate `invalid`, `voidable`, `not effective against a person`, `unauthorized but ratifiable`, and `breach after a valid act`.
- Limitation analysis is date-sensitive. Verify the current rule and any special limitation/exclusion period before stating a deadline.
- General Provisions often operate together with a specific book; the specific rule may control.
