# Marriage and Family / 婚姻家庭编 — issue map

## Main issue clusters
- Marriage formation/status and marital agreements.
- Spousal personal/property relations.
- Separate vs common property; property agreements.
- Spousal debts and third-party claims.
- Divorce routes and consequences.
- Child custody/parental responsibility, support and visitation.
- Property division and compensation/assistance/damages where legally applicable.
- Parent-child and other family relations; adoption where relevant.

## Intake facts
Marriage registration/date; children and caregiving; residence; property list; acquisition dates; funding; registration/title; loans/mortgages; debts and purpose; business interests; gifts/inheritance; agreements; major transfers; income; existing proceedings/orders; safety concerns.

## Property method
Create an asset/debt table. For each item record: description, acquisition date, title/registration, source of funds, current possession/control, debt/security, asserted classification, evidence, and dispute.

## Safety
If there is alleged domestic violence, threats, stalking, confinement or immediate personal danger, prioritize safety and available lawful protection channels before property optimization. Do not demand confrontation.

## Current-law check
Marriage/family judicial interpretations can materially affect property, debt, children and other issues. Verify the currently effective Supreme People's Court interpretations and amendments before giving article-level conclusions.

## Cautions
Do not assume registration in one spouse's name alone resolves ownership. Do not assume every debt incurred during marriage is automatically joint. Apply current rules to the specific acquisition, purpose, agreement and evidence.
