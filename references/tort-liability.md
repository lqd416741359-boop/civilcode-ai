# Tort Liability / 侵权责任编 — issue map

## Main issue clusters
- General tort elements and allocation of liability.
- Multiple actors, joint/several or apportioned liability where applicable.
- Personal injury and property damage remedies.
- Network infringement.
- Product liability.
- Motor-vehicle traffic accident interface.
- Medical injury.
- Environmental/ecological harm.
- Ultra-hazardous activities.
- Animal-caused damage.
- Building/object-related damage, falling objects and premises-type issues.
- Other special liability regimes in the Civil Code and special legislation.

## Intake facts
Protected interest/damage; exact conduct; actor/control relationship; time/place; fault facts where required; causation; alternative causes; warnings/safety measures; victim conduct; multiple actors; insurance; medical records; repair/appraisal; invoices; income-loss evidence; witness/video; police/authority records.

## Analysis sequence
1. Identify the protected interest and damage.
2. Identify applicable general or special liability rule.
3. Determine which elements require proof and who bears relevant burdens under current law.
4. Analyze causation and fault only as required by that regime.
5. Check victim conduct, multiple actors and third parties.
6. Identify remedies/damage items and evidence.

## Cautions
Do not apply a generic “fault + causation = liability” formula to every special tort. Some regimes use special presumptions or allocations that must be verified.
