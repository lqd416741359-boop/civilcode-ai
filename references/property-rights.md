# Property Rights / 物权编 — issue map

## Main issue clusters
- Ownership and changes in rights to movable/immovable property.
- Registration/delivery/publicity requirements and effects.
- Co-ownership and partition.
- Condominium/building ownership and owners' common rights.
- Neighbouring relations: access, drainage, ventilation/light, nuisance and similar issues.
- Usufructuary rights where relevant.
- Security interests: mortgage, pledge, lien and priority questions; check current judicial interpretations and related registration rules.
- Possession and protection of possession.

## Intake facts
Property type/location; registration/title holder; acquisition date; purchase/funding source; delivery/possession; contracts; registration changes; co-owners; mortgages/security; third-party transfers; notices; physical condition; disputed interference.

## Cross-routing
- Spouse ownership/debt -> also `marriage-family.md`.
- Estate/property after death -> also `inheritance.md`.
- Sale/lease/breach -> also `contracts.md`.
- Damage/leak/nuisance -> often also `tort-liability.md`.

## Cautions
Do not equate possession, payment, contract signature and registered ownership without checking the applicable property rule. Third-party good-faith acquisition, registration and security priority issues require precise fact and authority verification.
